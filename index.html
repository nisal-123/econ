How Campus Birthday Hub Works

Designed specifically for university departments to eliminate missed birthdays and manual spreadsheet chaos.

STEP 01

Student Submits Details

Students securely sign in, submit their birthday date, registration number, upload a clear profile photo (up to 5MB), and customize privacy consent.

STEP 02

Admin Reviews & Verifies

Department administrators verify identity, approve valid submissions, request corrections for blurry photos, and manage records securely.

STEP 03

Creative Post Studio

The designated Birthday Post Designer selects templates (Navy Gold, Soft Purple, Fiesta), customizes greetings, and exports ultra sharp 1080px PNGs.
